Are the juices actually fresh?

Yes. We cold-press our produce daily and keep every bottle chilled until it reaches you.

Can I come in without an order?

Absolutely. Walk-ins are welcome every day from 8 AM to 8 PM.

Do you have options for different diets?

Our menu includes vegan, gluten-free, and naturally sweetened options. Ask our team for today’s recommendations.

Can I bring my dog?

If they’re friendly and well-behaved, they’re more than welcome to join your Habibi Haven stop.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Delivery is available within the neighborhood during opening hours.
